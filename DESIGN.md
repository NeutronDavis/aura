## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a standard of quiet luxury, privacy, and curated intimacy for a high-tier adult companionship marketplace. Drawing visual codes from bespoke private aviation, discreet invitation-only supper clubs, and heritage hospitality ateliers, the interface rejects the frantic gamification, garish neon accents, and aggressive micro-animations typical of mainstream dating platforms. 

The aesthetic is grounded in warm minimalism paired with tactile editorial rigor. The visual space prioritizes emotional serenity, uncompromising discretion, and institutional-grade trust. Interactions feel deliberate, stately, and weightless, reassuring both patrons and companions through pristine typography, generous spatial composition, and meticulous hairline finishes.

## Colors

The palette relies on subtle chromatic warmth to evoke organic tactile textures like heavy cardstock, fine linen, and brushed architectural metals:

- **Primary (`#C5A059` / Champagne Bronze Gold):** Reserved for key focal actions, verified status accents, active states, and editorial highlights. Hover states shift down to `#B08D45`.
- **Secondary (`#1E6B4A` / Discreet Emerald):** Applied strictly to verification emblems, identity security shields, secure escrow badges, and biometric confirmation elements. Its supporting tinted surface is `#EBF5F0`.
- **Tertiary Surface (`#FAF5EB` / Pale Champagne):** Soft champagne wash used for pill tags, active selection backgrounds, and featured concierge ribbons.
- **Neutrals & Surfaces:**
  - Canvas Base: `#FAF9F6` (Alabaster Linen) transitioning to `#F5F3EF` for inset section containers.
  - Surface Card: Pure `#FFFFFF` to provide distinct crisp contrast against the linen backdrop.
  - Text Primary: `#131418` (Deep Obsidian Charcoal) for high legibility without the harshness of pure black.
  - Text Secondary: `#5C5E68` (Muted Slate) for supplementary copy, subtitles, and metadata.
  - Text Tertiary: `#8B8D97` (Warm Stone) for captions, structural indicators, and subtle timestamps.
  - Hairlines: `#EAE7E1` for primary card borders and `#E2DFD7` for focused or separating rules.

## Typography

The typographic tension pairs the literary warmth of `Newsreader` with the contemporary precision of `Plus Jakarta Sans`. 

Display and primary headlines employ `Newsreader` in normal weight with delicate tracking to echo private editorial monographs and luxury periodicals. Structural navigation, interactive triggers, metadata, profile details, and running copy utilize `Plus Jakarta Sans` to guarantee crisp legibility across high-density mobile interfaces. All small status labels, compliance markers (such as the 18+ badge), and verification indicators use `label-sm` with deliberate uppercase tracking to preserve clarity at tiny scales.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is constructed on an 8pt base spatial unit within a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop, scaling down to a single-column architecture bounded by comfortable edge gutters on mobile devices. 

Breathing room is treated as an intentional luxury material. Touch interfaces center on a 48px minimal touch-target policy. On mobile viewports, sticky reservation and booking action trays lock securely above the system gesture area with integrated safe-area padding (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`). Maximum desktop content containers are bound to 1280px to protect editorial line-lengths and deliberate pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy eschews heavy drop-shadows and synthetic blur effects in favor of quiet, multi-layered depth:

- **Surface Separation:** Depth is primarily established through tonal shifts (from `#FAF9F6` canvas to `#FFFFFF` floating card surfaces) defined with hairline borders (`1px solid #EAE7E1`).
- **Natural Ambient Diffusion:** Elevated interactive elements (such as modal sheets and sticky action docks) cast an ultra-soft, low-opacity warm shadow: `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(19, 20, 24, 0.04), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(19, 20, 24, 0.02)`.
- **Frost Overlays:** Bottom navigation bars and floating trays utilize an ultra-subtle translucent backing (`rgba(250, 249, 246, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`) framed by a hairline top stroke, allowing scrollable content to pass quietly underneath.

## Shapes

The shape grammar combines rounded architectural card profiles with soft organic pills. Standard profile cards and detail modules utilize a 16px to 20px radius (`rounded-lg` to `rounded-xl`), creating comfortable, thumb-friendly framing that avoids harsh clinical corners. 

Buttons, verification markers, filter tags, and compliance badges default to continuous curvature or full-pill profiles, maintaining a soft, approachable, and human posture throughout every viewport.

## Components

- **Buttons:** 
  - *Primary:* Champagne bronze solid background (`#C5A059`), deep obsidian charcoal text (`#131418`), full-pill radius, 48px height minimum for mobile touch safety.
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Hairline border (`#EAE7E1`), transparent background, `#131418` text, transitioning on hover/press to `#FAF5EB`.
- **Badges & Trust Shields:**
  - *18+ Compliance Badge:* Compact pill silhouette with hairline `#E2DFD7` border, `#FAF9F6` ground, `#5C5E68` label typography.
  - *Biometric & Identity Verification Shield:* Emerald background tint (`#EBF5F0`), deep emerald copy/icon (`#1E6B4A`), enclosing a refined vector shield motif with verified typography.
- **Cards & Profile Dossiers:**
  - Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface resting on the `#FAF9F6` canvas, bound by a 1px `#EAE7E1` hairline. 
  - Image ratios strictly follow a portrait editorial 3:4 aspect ratio with subtle bottom gradients for discrete typography overlays.
- **Form Fields & Inputs:**
  - Inset `#FAF9F6` background with a 1px `#EAE7E1` hairline border, rounding to 12px.
  - Active focus state softens the border to `#C5A059` with no jarring outer rings, complemented by `#131418` text and `#8B8D97` placeholders.
- **Mobile Bottom Navigation & Action Trays:**
  - Anchored bottom navigation height at 64px with blurred linen frost, housing 4 curated access points.
  - Detail screen booking trays float 12px above the viewport base, featuring a two-up structure: price/rate overview alongside an immediate full-pill primary engagement button.
